## Panel Prompts

Questions to guide the panel discussion:

1. What obligations do governments have to explain AI-assisted decisions that private companies may not have?
2. What does "open government" mean when some of the underlying AI technology is not open?
3. What safeguards are necessary before allowing AI-generated content to influence eligibility determinations or enforcement actions?
4. What governance structures are needed to oversee AI use across agencies consistently?
